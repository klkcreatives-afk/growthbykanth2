About Me

Hi, I’m Lakshmi Kanth, the creator of Growth by Kanth.

I’m passionate about trading, financial markets, and continuous learning, and I created this platform to share practical knowledge, market analysis, trading education, psychology, and risk-management principles with people who want to become more disciplined and informed traders.

I believe successful trading is not about chasing quick profits or predicting every market move—it’s about preparation, patience, risk management, discipline, and continuously improving your decision-making process.

Through Growth by Kanth, I’m building a community where traders can learn, practise, share ideas, review their decisions, and grow together. My goal is simple: to help you develop a better understanding of the markets and build a trading process you can follow with confidence and discipline.
